Psoriasis is a complex multifactorial skin disease affecting the population worldwide and is defined by the apparition of red plaques covered by white.

Psoriasis is a chronic, immune-mediated inflammatory skin disease. According to World Psoriasis Day Consortium 125 million people worldwide – 2-3% of global population have psoriasis.

Psoriasis has several variants, with plaque psoriasis representing approximately 80% to 90% of all manifestations of psoriasis.

Patients with psoriasis experience a deterioration in physical and mental functioning equivalent to that seen in other major chronic conditions, including cancer, cardiovascular disease, and diabetes.

None of the currently available treatments can cure psoriasis.

From treatment perspective psoriasis patients can be divided in two groups:

1

Patients requiring topical treatment

previously mild to moderate
2

Patients requiring systemic treatment

moderate to severe
